The mixed QA reactor covers 20 NH grid components but never forms a fluid multiblock or uses the five NH-only parts left out of its layout. This scenario builds the six-chamber vessel, loads those five parts, fills it with NH coolant and powers its port. The live run consumed 780 mB of coolant, produced 780 mB of hot coolant, reached 16,800 HU hull capacity and stored two breeder pulses.
