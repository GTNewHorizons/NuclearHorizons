It was revealed to me in a dream that spent fuel earns its final heat and energy step before replacement. Isolated lifecycle runs recorded that final output, while code review found NH could lose the step or leave spent fuel in place. The simulator now runs both phases per step. Measured reference limits set the hull to 10000 HU and reflector wear to 10000/40000. NH 0.0.9 used 30000/120000 reflector wear. This commit does not rescale saved wear, so old damaged reflectors may expire early. Maintainer review should decide whether migration is needed.
